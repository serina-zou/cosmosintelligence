Publication format

Each note should include:

  • a specific question
  • author or contributor team
  • published and last reviewed dates
  • source data or references
  • method
  • result
  • limitations
  • what would change the conclusion
  • related project pages

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Research status labels

Use visible labels such as Established observation, Research result, Active question, Hypothesis and Speculative idea where relevant.

This helps readers and AI systems avoid treating an interesting question as a proven claim.
