Bringing the cosmos to everyone

Humanity is entering a remarkable era of space exploration. Rockets are becoming more capable. Space telescopes are observing farther into the Universe. Artificial intelligence can analyze scientific data at scales that were previously impossible. New sensors detect signals our biological senses cannot perceive. Citizen scientists can participate in real research from almost anywhere on Earth.

Physical travel into space, however, will remain inaccessible to most people for a long time.

CosmosIntelligence begins with a different question:

What if we bring the cosmos to humanity?

Our goal is to make exploration of the Universe accessible to anyone with curiosity. A student should be able to explore Mars. A citizen scientist should be able to examine real astronomical data. A researcher should be able to compare signals across multiple observatories. A developer should be able to build tools around public space data. An entrepreneur should be able to discover opportunities in the emerging space economy.

Our mission

Aspire to Inspire. Help as many people as possible. Live Long and Prosper.

This mission guides CosmosIntelligence and the larger FloLabs ecosystem. We want to inspire curiosity, expand access to opportunity, help people learn by doing and create technologies that allow more people to participate in discoveries that can shape humanity's future.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Our vision

Bring space and the cosmos within reach of every human on Earth, transforming curiosity into exploration, participation, discovery and innovation.

Physical spaceflight is only one form of exploration. Humanity may not be able to send billions of people to the Moon, Mars, distant stars or other galaxies. Technology can increasingly allow billions of people to explore those places from Earth.

Through telescopes, spacecraft, public scientific archives, simulations, artificial intelligence, extended reality, advanced sensors and new forms of human computer interaction, the Universe can become increasingly accessible.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

What we believe

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

Curiosity is not limited by geography, credentials, age, wealth or professional title. Someone who begins as a student may become a researcher. Someone who joins as a citizen scientist may identify something unusual. Someone who starts by exploring Mars may become an engineer. Someone fascinated by an exoplanet may eventually build a company around a technology inspired by space research.

CosmosIntelligence is designed to widen the path from curiosity to contribution.

From spectators to explorers

Most people experience space as spectators. They watch launches, see photographs, read stories about discoveries and watch documentaries.

We want to change that relationship.

Imagine asking Space Buddy to show you Europa, then exploring its surface, seeing Europa Clipper, reviewing why scientists think an ocean may exist below the ice and moving directly into a citizen science or research pathway related to the topic.

Exploration becomes interactive. Learning becomes participatory. Curiosity becomes action.

A complement to physical space exploration

Government agencies and commercial space companies are working to return humans to the Moon, reach Mars and expand humanity's physical presence beyond Earth. That work is essential.

CosmosIntelligence approaches the same future from another direction. Physical exploration may involve thousands and eventually millions of people. Digital, scientific and educational exploration can involve billions.

A person should not need a spacecraft ticket to participate meaningfully in humanity's exploration of the Universe.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Curiosity can grow the space economy

The space economy will not grow only because rockets become cheaper. It will grow when more people care about space, learn about it, build around it and find useful ways to participate.

Curiosity becomes exploration. Exploration becomes learning. Learning becomes participation. Participation becomes innovation. Innovation expands the space economy.

By making space exploration meaningful to a much larger population, CosmosIntelligence can help widen the base of future scientists, engineers, researchers, entrepreneurs, educators, citizen scientists and customers.

Searching for life and intelligence

One of humanity's oldest questions remains unanswered: Are we alone?

Modern astronomy can study exoplanet atmospheres, chemical signatures, radio and optical signals, time varying observations and increasingly large scientific archives. AI can help examine datasets containing more information than people could inspect manually.

Future advances in AI, quantum science, neuroscience, photonics, communications, sensors and autonomous systems may give humanity tools we cannot currently imagine.

We do not claim to know whether extraterrestrial life or advanced civilizations exist. We believe the search is one of the most important explorations humanity can undertake.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Space Buddy as the human interface

NASA Eyes, Solar System Treks, astronomy archives, spacecraft data, 3D models, scientific papers, citizen science programs and future observation systems already contain extraordinary knowledge. The challenge is that they exist across many separate systems.

CosmosIntelligence can connect them. FloBrain provides intelligence, memory, context and tool coordination. AI Space View provides the visual universe canvas. Space Buddy becomes the conversational guide. The Cosmic Graph organizes relationships among objects, observations, events, signals, research and discoveries.

The future we want to help create

Humanity should keep building rockets, sending spacecraft farther into the Solar System, returning people to the Moon, exploring Mars and building better telescopes.

But participation in space does not have to wait for everyone to receive a seat on a spacecraft.

We can bring the cosmos to them.

We want a future where billions of people can explore, learn, contribute, discover, build and participate. A future where looking at the night sky does not make people feel distant from the Universe. It makes them feel part of it.
