Space Buddy product requirements and build map

Product direction: the capabilities and example conversations below describe planned Space Buddy work, not a released application.

This page makes the major product requirements public so contributors can see what the team is trying to build and identify useful work without relying on fixed calendar promises.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Product overview

Space Buddy is an AI powered interactive universe explorer. It combines a live sky, navigable 3D universe, conversational AI companion, NASA and scientific integrations, personalized memory, community participation, citizen science and immersive XR.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Core visual requirements

  • Accurate live sky based on selected location, date and time.
  • Adaptive observer viewpoints from supported celestial bodies.
  • Free roam navigation with understandable speed controls.
  • Progressive object interaction: hover, single click, deeper AI interaction.
  • Object self rotation for 3D inspection.
  • Clean visual mode that can hide interface panels.
  • Desktop and mobile layouts that keep the universe visible.

AI companion requirements

  • Text and two way audio.

  • Conversational navigation and tool routing.
  • Guided tours.
  • User controlled memory of interests, visits and unfinished work.
  • Personalized event and content recommendations.
  • Synchronized spoken responses and captions.

Integration requirements

  • NASA Eyes adapter using documented embeds and deep links.
  • Solar System Treks and selected map layers.
  • NASA 3D asset registry.
  • JPL Horizons connector.
  • NASA Exoplanet Archive TAP/API connector.
  • Guided citizen science handoffs.
  • Telescope workflows only through documented interfaces or formal partner access.

Community and engagement requirements

  • Optional shared universe focused on education and exploration.
  • Source aware space news feed.
  • Moderated community posts and astrophotography.

Research Hub

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

Work areas contributors can join

Initial work areas include the Space Experience Registry, NASA Eyes routing, sky engine evaluation, live sky prototype, planetary viewpoints, free roam navigation, voice interaction, source cards, exoplanet and Horizons connectors, Trek layers, XR assets, citizen science experiences, accessibility, analytics, community design, events, partner research, content, SEO and AI discovery, public demos and research publications.

Progress is measured by useful outputs and completion signals rather than a fixed timeline.

CosmosIntelligence / Research + product

What we are not doing

We are not rebuilding every NASA visualization from scratch. We are not relying on undocumented interfaces as core dependencies. We are not presenting the project as endorsed by NASA. We are not treating an anomaly score as scientific proof. We are not requiring VR to access core educational content.
