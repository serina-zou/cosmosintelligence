# CosmosIntelligence research

CosmosIntelligence is an open research and product initiative that uses public astronomy data and artificial intelligence to study cosmic structure, signals, anomalies and information patterns. The work is designed to produce useful science and technology whether or not any extraordinary hypothesis proves correct.

## Research pillars

### Mapping the cosmos

Study how Hubble, Roman, SPHEREx, Euclid, Gaia, SDSS, DESI and other surveys help humanity map an observable Universe that is far richer than unaided human vision suggests.

[Mapping the Cosmos](/research/mapping-the-cosmos/)

### The Cosmic Graph

Build a machine readable graph connecting objects, observations, signals, instruments, datasets, events, papers, anomalies and scientific relationships.

[Explore the Cosmic Graph](/research/cosmic-graph/)

### Cosmic intelligence and consciousness

Compare measurable network properties across the cosmic web, brains and other complex systems while treating consciousness as a research question rather than a predetermined conclusion.

[Cosmic Intelligence and Consciousness](/research/cosmic-intelligence-consciousness/)

### Search for life and intelligence

Study biosignatures, technosignatures, exoplanet observations, SETI methods, multimodal anomaly search and the long term question of communication with other intelligence.

[Search for Life and Intelligence](/research/search-for-life-intelligence/)

### Evidence standards

Learn normal astrophysics before searching for unusual patterns. Require provenance, known physics controls, statistical testing, replication, prediction where possible and independent review.

[Evidence and Research Standards](/research/evidence-standards/)

## The research engine

The research architecture combines an open science data lake, multimodal embeddings, the Cosmic Graph, anomaly and information models, control filters, validation workflows and a candidate review dashboard.

The model stack can include vision models for survey images, spectral models for emission and absorption features, time series models for light curves and pulses, graph models for cosmic web structure, and information models for entropy, compression and recurring structure.
