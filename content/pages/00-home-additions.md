# Homepage additions

The existing homepage already establishes the research engine, AI Space View, Space Buddy, evidence standards and contributor pathways. Keep those sections. Add the following blocks so visitors and search systems can reach the deeper pages without turning the homepage into an encyclopedia.

## Explore the bigger questions

### Why CosmosIntelligence exists

We want to bring space and the cosmos within reach of every human on Earth. Learn how our mission, vision and belief connect scientific exploration, experiential learning and participation in the space economy.

[Read why CosmosIntelligence exists](/why-cosmosintelligence/)

### Mapping the cosmos

The night sky is not empty. It is nature continuing beyond Earth. Explore how Hubble, Roman, SPHEREx and other surveys are changing our ability to map stars, galaxies, signals and cosmic structure.

[Explore Mapping the Cosmos](/research/mapping-the-cosmos/)

### Cosmic intelligence and consciousness

Can similar mathematical patterns appear in brains, biological networks and the cosmic web? We treat consciousness as a research question, not a conclusion, and test measurable structures with explicit scientific controls.

[Explore the research frontier](/research/cosmic-intelligence-consciousness/)
