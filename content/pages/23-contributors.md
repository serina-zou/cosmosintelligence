# Contributors

CosmosIntelligence aims to make useful work visible and credit contributors who choose to be public. Public profiles will appear here as contributors approve their details.

## Contributor profiles can include

* name and preferred public identity
* role or workstream
* research interests
* skills
* authored research notes
* product features or integrations contributed to
* demos or public presentations
* citizen science participation
* external professional links where the contributor chooses to share them

## Why this matters

Authorship creates accountability and helps visitors understand who produced a piece of work. It also gives contributors a concrete portfolio of research, product and communication outputs.

Contributor profiles should never imply credentials or institutional affiliations that have not been verified.
