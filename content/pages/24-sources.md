## Source policy

Technical claims should link to the closest useful primary source. Secondary reporting can provide context but should not replace the underlying research or official mission source when that source is available.

Sources should be reviewed periodically because APIs, mission status, URLs and scientific interpretations can change.

## Independence statement

CosmosIntelligence and Space Buddy are independent projects. Use of public NASA or other scientific resources does not imply sponsorship, endorsement or partnership.
