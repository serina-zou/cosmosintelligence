# Space Buddy integrations

Space Buddy is designed as a router, guide and research companion. It should connect to high quality existing scientific resources instead of rebuilding every visualization, map or database from scratch.

## Core visual resources

### NASA Eyes

Interactive 3D views of the Solar System, Earth, asteroids, exoplanets and spacecraft. NASA documents website embedding and deep links for many Eyes experiences.

[NASA Eyes](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/)  
[How Space Buddy can integrate NASA Eyes](/product/space-buddy/integrations/nasa-eyes/)

### Solar System Treks

Planetary surface exploration, imagery, terrain and geospatial services for selected worlds.

[Solar System Treks](https://trek.nasa.gov/)  
[Planetary Maps and 3D](/product/space-buddy/integrations/planetary-maps-3d/)

### NASA 3D Resources

Downloadable NASA mission and science models that can support native AR, WebXR and VR experiences.

[NASA 3D Resources](https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/)

## NASA and affiliated XR experiences

* Spacecraft AR
* ClipperAR
* First Woman
* Reach Across the Stars
* Walking Among the Stars
* NASA Home and City

These experiences should initially be treated as launchable or linkable resources unless a documented integration interface is available.

## Research grade data

### JPL Horizons

Positions, ephemerides and observational geometry for Solar System objects and spacecraft.

[JPL Horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/)

### NASA Exoplanet Archive

Confirmed planet data, candidate data and programmatic TAP access.

[NASA Exoplanet Archive](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/)  
[Scientific Data Connectors](/product/space-buddy/integrations/scientific-data/)

## Citizen science

### NASA Exoplanet Watch

A public citizen science program where participants can analyze or collect real exoplanet transit data. Participants without a telescope can use remote or archived data.

[NASA Exoplanet Watch](https://science.nasa.gov/citizen-science/exoplanet-watch/)  
[Citizen Science with Space Buddy](/citizen-science/)

## Integration principle

Use documented interfaces. Preserve attribution. Keep external resources behind adapters. Provide a clean fallback when an experience cannot be embedded. Never imply NASA endorsement unless a formal relationship exists.
