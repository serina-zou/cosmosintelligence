# Contributor roles

CosmosIntelligence needs a multidisciplinary team because the project combines science, software, education, community and commercial development.

## Research and science

**Astronomy and astrophysics:** source validation, catalog realism, mission science, datasets and interpretation.

**Research associates:** methods, literature reviews, experiment design, validation and research notes.

**Citizen scientists:** participation testing, data review, classification, observing workflows and public research contribution.

**Astrobiology and SETI research:** biosignatures, technosignatures, exoplanets, signals and search methods.

**Neuroscience and complexity:** network comparisons, information theory, consciousness research questions and cross scale methods.

## AI, software and data

**AI and machine learning:** multimodal models, embeddings, routing, anomaly detection and evaluation.

**Data engineering:** astronomy archives, ETL, metadata, reproducibility and graph pipelines.

**Software engineering:** web, APIs, connectors, services, authentication and infrastructure.

**3D, geospatial and XR:** sky rendering, navigation, planetary maps, models, WebXR, AR and VR.

**QA and accessibility:** scientific accuracy, product testing, assistive technology and inclusive interfaces.

## Product and design

**Product management:** requirements, work areas, user research, prioritization and shipped outcomes.

**UX and visual design:** universe navigation, interaction models, mobile behavior, research dashboards and public pages.

**Analytics:** engagement, route success, search behavior, experiment measurement and product learning.

## Business and growth

**Business development:** museums, schools, observatories, universities, science centers, commercial partners and new use cases.

**Partnerships:** relationship mapping, outreach, collaboration structures and integration opportunities.

**Marketing:** positioning, launch campaigns, audience development, newsletters, social channels and community growth.

**Search and AI discovery:** SEO, internal linking, structured data, source architecture, AI citation monitoring and content performance.

**Funding research:** grants, foundations, sponsorships, educational funding and relevant programs.

## Content, education and community

**Science communication:** explainers, research summaries, interviews, scripts and public source notes.

**Education:** guided experiences, curriculum concepts, educator resources and learning evaluation.

**Community:** contributor onboarding, events, moderation, citizen science support and engagement.

**Media:** demos, video, audio, diagrams, astrophotography and transcripts.

**Localization:** multilingual content, international outreach and cultural adaptation.

## Operations and governance

**Project operations:** task systems, documentation, coordination and process improvement.

**Legal and compliance research:** licensing, media use, privacy, community policy and partner requirements.

**Trust and safety:** moderation, reporting, identity and age appropriate community controls.

If your discipline is not listed but you can identify a concrete way to move the research, product or participation ecosystem forward, there may still be a useful role.
