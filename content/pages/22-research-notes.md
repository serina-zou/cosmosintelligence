# Research notes

Research notes will document original experiments, methods, findings and limitations. No completed research notes are published on this page yet.

## What belongs here

* connector evaluations
* dataset maps
* literature reviews
* benchmark results
* false positive studies
* network comparisons
* anomaly reviews
* negative results
* usability research
* citizen science walkthroughs
* telescope integration tests
* source comparisons
* prototype findings
* technical architecture notes

## Publication format

Each note should include:

* a specific question
* author or contributor team
* published and last reviewed dates
* source data or references
* method
* result
* limitations
* what would change the conclusion
* related project pages

## Research status labels

Use visible labels such as **Established observation**, **Research result**, **Active question**, **Hypothesis** and **Speculative idea** where relevant.

This helps readers and AI systems avoid treating an interesting question as a proven claim.
