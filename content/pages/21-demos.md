# Demos

These are planned demonstration briefs. No working Space Buddy demos are published on this page yet.

## Planned demo sequence

### Europa guided exploration

Begin with “Take me to Europa.” Open the relevant NASA Eyes view, explain Europa, move to Europa Clipper, show the mission context and then offer a related research or learning path.

### Live sky

Show the same time and object from two Earth locations, then move the observer to the Moon and compare the view.

### Exoplanet to citizen science

Open an exoplanet, query structured data, explain a transit and hand the user into NASA Exoplanet Watch.

### Voice exploration

Demonstrate hands free navigation, spoken explanations and synchronized captions.

### Planetary terrain

Move from a global Mars view into terrain and measurements through Solar System Treks or mapped layers.

### XR

Place a NASA spacecraft or rover model into a room, inspect it and ask Space Buddy to explain an instrument.

## Demo publishing standards

Every demo should include a transcript, source links, a short explanation of what is real versus simulated, the contributor team and a last reviewed date.
