# Inner-page carousel audit

Audited all 174 image-and-text sections across 25 inner pages at desktop widths of 1440 and 1024 pixels. Home is excluded.

24 sections on 13 pages exceeded 420 pixels of text height and 1.5 times the image height at the narrower desktop width. These sections use horizontal text carousels; shorter sections retain their existing layout. Selection is stored in `content/carousels.json`.

Slides preserve heading-and-description groups, paragraph order, list numbering, and links. They do not auto-advance. Controls support horizontal scrolling, touch swipes, previous/next buttons, and keyboard arrows/Home/End when the track is focused. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Without JavaScript, all slides remain visible in reading order.
